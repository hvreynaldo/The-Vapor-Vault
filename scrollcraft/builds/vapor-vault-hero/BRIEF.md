# The Vapor Vault - Landing page

**Status:** Self-authored working brief based on the approved implementation plan and the user's direction to continue with the V0 hero and adapt the proven remaining page structure from Eureka.

## What is known

- The Vapor Vault is a new adult-only smoke shop in Beaumont, Texas, with a deliberately smaller opening assortment.
- The first deliverable is a premium one-page landing page, not a catalog or inventory system.
- `references/V0.mp4` is the creative proof: logo, vault mechanism, Volt entering the frame, portal transition, and the real storefront.
- The visitor must never see Volt using a product, vaping, smoke, product claims, prices, flavors, or availability promises.

## Authored direction

This is a **filmic one-shot**. The scroll-driven V0 clip does the narrative work; the rest of the page resolves that film into a calm, practical visit experience.

Feeling curve: **curiosity** at the vault mark → **awe** as Volt crosses the threshold → **recognition** at the real storefront → **confidence** through interior reference imagery → **warmth** around Volt and the new-store message → **resolve** through one directions action.

The signature move is the **Vault Dial**: a quiet mechanical ring that gains tension as the sequence advances and frames the directions action. It is decorative context, not a visible progress indicator.

## Content choices

- Headline: “Enter The Vapor Vault.”
- Supporting line: “A new smoke-shop experience, built around the real Beaumont storefront.”
- Interior: two available store reference images, presented as prototype media pending owner authorization.
- Selection: intentionally avoids unconfirmed categories, products, prices, stock, health claims, flavors, and e-commerce. It communicates an in-store, focused opening range only.
- Guardian: Volt is a store-level character and is never paired with, holding, or endorsing a product.
- New-store message: describes the real storefront and the in-person experience without invented reviews, age, sales history, or operating hours.
- Primary action: “Get directions” to `6080 Muela Creek Dr Unit 1, Beaumont, TX 77706`.
- Secondary action: “Call the store” at `(409) 225-5827`.
- Age access: a lightweight 21+ confirmation shown before interaction, persisted only in the current browser.

## Asset and device contract

- `vault-portal-desktop.mp4` is a silent, dense-keyframe web encode of the approved V0 source.
- `vault-portal-poster.webp` holds the first paint until an actual video frame is ready.
- `vault-portal-mobile-crop.mp4` is a **temporary center-cropped 9:16 fallback** for a usable phone-first composition. It is not final mobile art direction and must be replaced with a purpose-composed portrait master before launch.
- Reduced-motion visitors receive the poster and the same copy, location, and directions action without video fetching.
- The interior, storefront, and Volt still assets are prototype references. They must be replaced or explicitly owner-approved before public launch.
